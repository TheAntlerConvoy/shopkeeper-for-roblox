Shopkeeper for Roblox

A browser extension that adds bulk tools for game passes and developer products to the Roblox Creator Hub.

Add to Chrome Free for up to 3 items at a time, with a 3-day Pro trial. Coming soon to Microsoft Edge.

What it does

  • Creates many game passes or developer products at once, from a preset or your own list, with names, prices, descriptions and images.
  • Changes prices across a game by a percentage or an amount, puts items on or off sale, renames them and edits their descriptions.
  • Shows a preview of every change before it is made, and saves a history so any bulk change can be undone.
  • Estimates the Robux each game pass has earned.
  • Searches names, descriptions and IDs, and filters by sale status and price (Pro).
  • Copies game passes and developer products, with their icons, into another of your experiences (Pro).
  • Exports every game pass and developer product to CSV, with prices and game pass sales, and imports changes from a CSV file (Pro).
  • Exports IDs and prices as a Luau ModuleScript for your purchase scripts in Roblox Studio (Pro).

Security

Shopkeeper never reads, stores or sends your .ROBLOSECURITY cookie, your password or any other account data. It only runs on create.roblox.com, it only talks to Roblox's own servers and to ExtensionPay (to check whether you have paid for Pro), and it has no server of its own. The privacy policy explains the details.
